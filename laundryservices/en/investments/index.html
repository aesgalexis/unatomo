Investment decisions based on technical judgement and a plant-wide view

Review an investment

Before committing the budget

The best machine is not always the best solution

An investment must fit volumes, product mix, shifts, staffing, utilities and the equipment already in place. A technical data sheet alone cannot explain that fit.

We provide an independent assessment, without representing brands or receiving manufacturer commissions. We start with the laundry's needs and the result the project must deliver.

What we review

From the need to a viable project

We compare the commercial proposal with real operations to identify hidden costs, dependencies and decisions that should be settled before signing.

Need, capacity and sizing

We define the problem the investment must solve and the useful capacity the plant needs, avoiding both bottlenecks and oversizing.

  • Hourly output, peaks, shifts and expected growth
  • Compatibility with upstream and downstream processes
  • Flexibility for changes in customers, formats or linen types

Technical comparison of proposals

We organise bids using comparable criteria and review performance, scope, exclusions, warranties and service commitments.

  • Expected output, quality, automation and consumption
  • Maintenance, spare parts, support and expected service life
  • Installation, testing and acceptance conditions

Integration and project costs

A purchase rarely ends with the machine itself. We review building work, utilities, internal transport, safety, data and the changes required to put it into production.

  • Electricity, steam, air, water, extraction and available space
  • Ancillary work, access, flows and adaptation of other equipment
  • Total implementation, operating and maintenance cost

Commissioning and verification

We can support installation and verify that the equipment delivers what was agreed under real conditions, with defined responsibilities, measurements and acceptance criteria.

When to involve us

Before a decision that is difficult to reverse is final

  • There are several bids and comparing them on equal terms is difficult.
  • The promised capacity has not been checked against the actual production mix.
  • The project may require changes to utilities, flows or existing equipment.
  • There are questions about after-sales service, spare parts or maintenance cost.
  • The investment is strategic and an independent second opinion is needed.
