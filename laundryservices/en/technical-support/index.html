From faults to full overhauls

We work on the complete machine

We work with machinery in industrial laundries, on-premise laundries and dry cleaners: batch washers, presses, washer-extractors, dryers, ironers, feeders, folders and auxiliary equipment.

The intervention can range from diagnostics and repair to a complete equipment overhaul, covering its mechanical, electrical, electronic and control systems.

What we do

Resolve, recover and upgrade

Diagnostics and repair

We find the source of faults and recurring failures, adjust the machine and restore stable operation.

  • Mechanical, electrical, electronic and automation work
  • Adjustments, modifications and commissioning
  • Planned interventions and complex troubleshooting

Complete overhauls and proprietary systems

We can completely overhaul batch washers, presses, dryers and other equipment, and install our own control system adapted to the machine and process.

  • Renewal of control panels, control components and wiring
  • Programming, operator interface and signal management
  • Commissioning, testing and overhaul documentation
