A plant-wide view

Performance depends on the whole plant

Equipment, people, time, consumption and maintenance are part of the same process. When one part does not fit, production loses stability and capacity.

We bring an independent technical view of the whole plant. We observe real operations, identify the source of problems and prioritise actions that can be implemented and verified.

Where we add value

From diagnosis to improvement

The audit is our recommended starting point. Our other services allow us to support the decisions and actions that emerge from the analysis.

Technical audit and process improvement

We inspect the laundry in operation to identify bottlenecks, recurring faults, operational imbalances and opportunities to improve production and maintenance.

The outcome: a technical report organised by area with clear priorities—what needs urgent correction, what should be scheduled and which investments are worth considering.

  • Flows, timing and actual production capacity
  • Equipment condition, settings and coordination
  • Preventive maintenance, critical spares and working practices

Technical assistance and problem solving

We diagnose faults and deviations that require more than replacing a part. We work on the machine while considering its impact on the rest of the process.

  • Electrical, mechanical, electronic and process diagnosis
  • Adjustments, configuration, commissioning and modifications
  • Scheduled technical operations and specialist coordination

Investment and equipment

We review needs, proposals and specifications before a purchase is closed. We assess the machine, after-sales service and its fit with real operations, without representing brands or taking manufacturer commissions.

  • Capacity, flexibility, automation and consumption
  • Maintenance, spare parts, after-sales service and useful life
  • Process integration and any additional changes required

Productivity and digital tools

We capture the information needed to understand production and make decisions based on real data, without imposing a closed system or unnecessarily changing the way people work.

  • Piece counters by linen type or customer
  • Machine or line activity, performance and signal capture
  • Integration with new or existing databases

When to call us

When the plant is producing, but you know it could perform better

  • A fault or deviation keeps returning without its root cause being found.
  • Production, maintenance and suppliers offer different explanations.
  • The machinery works, but the complete process is not stable.
  • A major investment is being prepared without an independent technical opinion.
  • Available data does not explain what happens during the working day.
  • Several separate problems need to become one action plan.
